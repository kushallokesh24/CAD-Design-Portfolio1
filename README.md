## Overview
A collection of 3D parametric mechanical parts modeled in **SolidWorks**, demonstrating multi-plane sketching, symmetric extrusions, tapered profiles, concentric bores, and precision slot/keyway cuts.

---

## 1. Flanged Tapered Sleeve / Bushing with Keyway Slot
![Flanged Tapered Sleeve](flanged_tapered_sleeve.png)
- **Features Used:** Cylindrical base flange extrusion, tapered/chamfered upper collar, concentric through-bore, and axial rectangular keyway slot cut.
- **Design Focus:** Concentricity, shaft-hub alignment, and keyway locating geometry for mechanical power transmission components.

---

## 2. Slotted Wedge / Tapered Support Block
![Slotted Wedge Support Block](slotted_wedge_support.png)
- **Features Used:** Rectangular base plate extrusion, inclined triangular profile extrusion, and internal U-slot cut.
- **Design Focus:** Multi-profile sketching across perpendicular faces and controlled material removal for structural support brackets.

---

## 3. 3-Axis Orthogonal Interlocking Cross Joint
![3-Axis Cross Joint](orthogonal_cross_joint.png)
- **Features Used:** Multi-plane sketching (Front, Top, and Right reference planes) combined with symmetric mid-plane extrusions.
- **Design Focus:** Spatial coordinate alignment and symmetric feature modeling across orthogonal X, Y, and Z axes.
